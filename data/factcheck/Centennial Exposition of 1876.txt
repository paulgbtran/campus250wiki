* **Susan B. Anthony's Protest Detail (Course of Events):** Corrected the description of Susan B. Anthony's July 4 protest. She did not read the "Declaration of Rights for Women" on the main official stage; she presented the document to Vice President Thomas W. Ferry on stage and then stepped down to read it aloud to the assembled crowd from a nearby bandstand/music stand in Independence Square.
* **Frederick Douglass Security Detail (Course of Events):** Changed "Capitol police" to "police officers". The United States Capitol Police operate exclusively in Washington, D.C. The security at the Philadelphia exposition grounds consisted of local police and the temporary Centennial Guard.
