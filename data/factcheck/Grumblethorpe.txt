* **Corrected Agnew Shooting Location**: Changed the location where British Brigadier General James Agnew was shot from "Germantown Avenue and Washington Lane" to near the Lower Burying Ground (Hood's Cemetery) along Germantown Avenue. Hood's Cemetery is situated in Lower Germantown (4900 block of Germantown Ave), whereas Washington Lane is located significantly further north (6300 block).
* **Corrected Architectural Façade Description and ASCII Diagram**: Changed the front façade description from "three-bay" to "five-bay" and updated the accompanying ASCII diagram. Grumblethorpe was built as a five-bay Georgian structure (five windows across the upper story and four windows flanking a central door on the ground floor), which earned it the nickname "Wister's Big House."
