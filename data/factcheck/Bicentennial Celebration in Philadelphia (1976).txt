* **Actual Attendance**: Corrected actual tourism attendance figures from "14–20 million" to "4–5 million" (summer of 1976) in the Infobox, Lead section, Aftermath section, and Outcomes diagram. While early projections ranged between 15 and 45 million (or up to 100 million for an international world's fair), actual summer visitation to Philadelphia reached only about 4 to 5 million people.
* **Key Organizers**: Removed the unverified name "Catherine Sue Leslie" from the Infobox and Background section, rephrasing the sentence to accurately reflect that "Agenda for Action" was a scattered-site plan developed by community organizations and the Bicentennial Corporation.
* **Sara Lee Birthday Cake Weight**: Corrected the weight of the Sara Lee Bicentennial cake served at Memorial Hall from 50,000 pounds to 76,000 pounds (crafted specifically in honor of '76).
* **Original Liberty Bell Casting Year**: Corrected the casting year of the original Liberty Bell from 1751 to 1752. The Pennsylvania Assembly ordered the bell in 1751, but the Whitechapel Bell Foundry cast it in London in 1752.
