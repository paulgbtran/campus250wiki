* **Architectural Style**: Changed "Georgian" to "Federal" style throughout the article (Infobox, Lead, Section 4). While built to harmonize with the Georgian style of Independence Hall, Old City Hall itself was constructed in 1790–1791 in the Federal architectural style.
* **Primary Historical Uses & Lead/History Sections**: Corrected the reference describing the building as an "Immigration and Naturalization Center (Late 18th Century)." Specialized federal immigration processing centers did not exist in the 18th century; Old City Hall functioned as a venue where naturalization oaths and court proceedings took place before local and federal courts.
* **Philadelphia's First City Hall / Courthouse Details**: Clarified that Philadelphia's earlier municipal building (built 1707–1710) was the Old Courthouse (or Town House) located at 2nd and High (Market) Streets.
* **1742 Election Riot**: Corrected the description of the 1742 Election Riot at the Old Courthouse. It was a gang of sailors allied with the Proprietary Party who attacked the Quaker/Country Party voters on the balcony stairs, not the other way around.
* **Chisholm v. Georgia Case Details**: Corrected the description of *Chisholm v. Georgia* (1793). The case did not involve "South Carolina land speculators," but rather Alexander Chisholm, executor of the estate of South Carolina merchant Robert Farquhar, suing Georgia over unpaid debts for military supplies furnished during the Revolutionary War.
