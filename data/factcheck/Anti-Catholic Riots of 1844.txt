* **Corrected facility destroyed from "seminary" to "convent" (Infobox, Lead section, and Kensington section):** St. Charles Borromeo Seminary was not burned during the riots; the religious institution destroyed in Kensington on May 8 alongside St. Michael's Church was the Sisters of Charity convent (which operated a school and dwelling). 
* **Corrected age of George Shiffler (May Riots section):** Changed "19-year-old" to "18-year-old". George Shiffler was born on January 24, 1826, and was 18 years old when he was killed on May 6, 1844.
