* **Early life and education**: Corrected details regarding Sun Ra's World War II conscientious objector history. The draft board granted him conscientious objector status (4-E); he was arrested and jailed because he refused assignment to report to a Civilian Public Service camp. He was later granted a 4-F medical discharge.
* **Career (New York era)**: Corrected the timeline regarding the Minimoog synthesizer. The Minimoog was not released until late 1970 (Sun Ra first tested prototype Moog synthesizers in 1969), so citing it under his 1961–1968 NYC period was anachronistic. Clarified his use of earlier electronic keyboards during the 1960s (e.g., Clavioline) and noted his later adoption of Moog synthesizers in 1969.
* **Personal life**: Corrected Sun Ra's burial site from Shadow Lawn Memorial Park to Elmwood Cemetery in Birmingham, Alabama.
