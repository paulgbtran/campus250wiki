* **Background & Lead Sections / ASCII Flowchart**: Corrected "Spanish-American War" to "Philippine-American War". The Spanish-American War formally ended in 1898; Fritz Scheel conducted the two benefit concerts in March 1900 to raise funds for the families of U.S. soldiers fighting in the Philippine-American War.
* **ASCII Flowchart**: Updated the flowchart entry label to "Spring 1900: Philippine-American War Relief" to accurately reflect the timing of the war relief concerts.
* **Course of Events Section**: Corrected "six pair concerts" to "six concerts". The orchestra's inaugural 1900–1901 season consisted of six single concerts, rather than six concert pairs.
* **Course of Events Section (Program Repertoire)**: Corrected the program details for the inaugural concert on November 16, 1900. The actual performed repertoire consisted of Karl Goldmark's Overture *In Spring* (*Im Frühling*), Ludwig van Beethoven's Symphony No. 5 in C minor, and Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky's Piano Concerto No. 1 in B-flat minor (featuring soloist Ossip Gabrilowitsch). The original text incorrectly listed Beethoven's *Egmont* Overture, Tchaikovsky's 5th Symphony, Goldmark's 1st Violin Concerto with Fritz Kreisler, and Weber's *Der Freischütz* Overture.
