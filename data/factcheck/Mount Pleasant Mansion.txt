* **Infobox & Lead Section & History Section**: Corrected the National Historic Landmark designation date from July 24, 1961 to October 15, 1966. (Mount Pleasant was officially designated an NHL on October 15, 1966, the same date it was entered onto the National Register of Historic Places).
* **Lead Section & Architecture Section**: Corrected the dependency building description from "single-story" to "two-story".
* **History Section**: Corrected the post-Revolutionary ownership sequence. Edward Shippen did not purchase the property in 1796. Following the confiscation of Benedict Arnold's property, merchant Blair McClenachan purchased Mount Pleasant in 1784, and General Jonathan Williams purchased it in 1792 (not 1803).
* **Cultural Significance & See Also Sections**: Removed *Cliveden* from the list of Fairmount Park historic houses. Cliveden is located in Germantown and is owned/managed by the National Trust for Historic Preservation / Historic Germantown, not Fairmount Park. Replaced with *Laurel Hill*, an actual Fairmount Park historic mansion.
