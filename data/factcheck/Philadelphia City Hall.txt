* **Architecture Section (William Penn Statue Details):** Removed incorrect statements regarding John Cheere's 1774 lead statue of William Penn at Pennsylvania Hospital (which was originally sculpted for an English garden at West Wycombe Park, not designed to sit atop a building). Clarified that Alexander Milne Calder independently designed the proportions and downward gaze of the statue for perspective viewing from the ground, and added the historical detail that Calder intended the statue to face south for optimal solar lighting, but it was installed facing northeast toward Penn Treaty Park.
