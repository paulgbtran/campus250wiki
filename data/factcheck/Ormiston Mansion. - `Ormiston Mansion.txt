* **Lead section**: Corrected the architectural description of Ormiston from having an "expansive wraparound wooden porch" to "an expansive wooden porch facing the river." Ormiston features a prominent porch along its riverfront elevation rather than a wraparound porch.
* **Early life and education**: Corrected Edward Burd's Revolutionary War military command. Burd served as a captain under Colonel William Montgomery's battalion in the Pennsylvania Flying Camp, not Colonel Nicholas Haussegger's battalion (Haussegger commanded the German Regiment).
* **Personal life**: Clarified Edward Burd's relationship to Margaret "Peggy" Shippen. Because Burd married Elizabeth Shippen (Peggy Shippen's sister), he was Peggy Shippen's brother-in-law (as well as her first cousin), rather than a "cousin-in-law."
