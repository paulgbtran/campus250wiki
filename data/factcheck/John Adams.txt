* **Personal Life section**: Corrected the statement that five of John and Abigail Adams's six children survived to adulthood to "four of whom survived to adulthood". Susanna died as a toddler in 1770 (aged 1) and Elizabeth was stillborn in 1777; only Abigail, John Quincy, Charles, and Thomas Boylston lived to adulthood.
* **Lead / Career sections**: Clarified that Adams did not serve his vice presidency *entirely* in Philadelphia from 1789 to 1797. The national capital was located in New York City from April 1789 until December 1790, at which point the government relocated to Philadelphia under the Residence Act.
