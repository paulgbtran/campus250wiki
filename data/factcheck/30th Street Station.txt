* **Lead Section (Location)**: Corrected the station's geographical placement from "between Market Street and Chestnut Street" to "between Market Street and Arch Street". Market Street forms the southern boundary of 30th Street Station, whereas Chestnut Street lies two blocks south.
* **Lead Section & Public Art (Memorial Description)**: Corrected the depiction of Walker Hancock's *Angel of Resurrection* (*Pennsylvania Railroad World War II Memorial*) from "the Archangel Michael" to "an angel" (or "Angel of Resurrection"), which accurately reflects sculptor Walker Hancock's design and official Association for Public Art records.
* **Public Art and Memorials (Material)**: Corrected the material description of Karl Bitter's 1895 bas-relief *The Spirit of Transportation* from "plaster" to "terracotta".
