* **Corrected Circumstances of Assassination**: Corrected the narrative regarding Catto's murder on October 10, 1871. Catto had already cast his ballot earlier in the morning and was returning to his home at 814 South Street in the afternoon after purchasing a handgun for self-defense due to ongoing street violence when he was shot.
* **Corrected Assassination Location**: Updated location of the shooting from 9th and South Streets to near 8th and South Streets (near his residence on South Street).
* **Specified Political Background of Killer and Trial Details**: Clarified that Frank Kelly was a Democratic party operative associated with local party boss William McMullen (rather than simply a generic "white supremacist"), and added that Kelly escaped after the shooting, was tried in 1877, and acquitted by an all-white jury.
* **Corrected W.E.B. Du Bois Quote**: Corrected the misquoted text attributed to W.E.B. Du Bois to his actual quote from *The Philadelphia Negro* (1899): *"Thus died Octavius V. Catto, a man of splendid equipment, rare force of character, and broad culture, who fell a sacrifice to mob passion."*
