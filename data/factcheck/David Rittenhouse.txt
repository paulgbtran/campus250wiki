* **Infobox and Personal life section**: Corrected David Rittenhouse's first wife's name from "Eleanor Coulston" to "Eleanor Barton" (daughter of Bernard Barton), whom he married on February 20, 1766.
* **Astronomical Pursuits and the 1769 Transit of Venus section**: Replaced "Thomas Coombe" with "John Sellers". The American Philosophical Society committee appointed to observe the transit at Norriton consisted of Rittenhouse, Rev. Dr. William Smith, John Lukens, and John Sellers.
* **Political Career and Public Administration section**: Corrected the department overseer of the U.S. Mint in 1792 from the "Department of the Treasury" to the "Department of State". Under the Coinage Act of 1792, the U.S. Mint initially fell under the authority of the Secretary of State (Thomas Jefferson) and was not formally transferred to the Treasury Department until 1873.
* **Legacy section**: Corrected "USS *Rittenhouse*: An armed steam vessel utilized during the nineteenth century" to "SS *David Rittenhouse*: A World War II Liberty ship constructed in 1943." There was no 19th-century armed steam vessel named USS *Rittenhouse*.
