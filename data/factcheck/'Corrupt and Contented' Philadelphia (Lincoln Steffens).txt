* **Lead Section**: Corrected the series installment number. "Philadelphia: Corrupt and Contented" was the **fourth** installment in Lincoln Steffens' *McClure's Magazine* series on municipal corruption (following St. Louis, Minneapolis, and Pittsburgh), not the fifth.
* **Career Section**: Corrected the description of Steffens' findings regarding demographic composition and political corruption. Steffens argued that Philadelphia was predominantly populated by native-born Americans of old Anglo-Saxon stock, using this fact to refute the contemporary claim that municipal corruption was caused by recent foreign immigrants.
* **Legacy Section & Diagram**: Corrected the duration of continuous Republican control of the Philadelphia mayoralty. Republican control was not continuous for 85 years (1867–1951), as Democratic Mayor Samuel G. King served from 1881 to 1884. The unbroken period of Republican mayors ran for 67 years (1884–1952, ending when Democrat Joseph S. Clark Jr. took office in January 1952 following his November 1951 election).
* **Legacy Section**: Added state jurisdictional clarification to Wayne Bryant, noting that he was a New Jersey State Senator (representing Camden County in South Jersey), whereas Vincent Fumo was a Pennsylvania State Senator representing Philadelphia.
