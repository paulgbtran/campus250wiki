* **Anti-Slavery Society Office Address:** Corrected the address from "105 N. Fifth St." to "107 N. Fifth St." in the Infobox, Development section, and ASCII routing diagram. (Under pre-1857 Philadelphia street numbering, the office address was 31 N. Fifth St., renumbered to 107 N. Fifth St.).
* **Origin of "Underground Railroad" Term:** Corrected "late 1780s" to "around 1831" in the Origins section. Commercial steam rail transport did not exist in the 1780s; historical accounts (including Robert Smedley's 1883 history) trace the origin of the term in Columbia, PA to around 1831 (following the escape of Tice Davids).
* **Larry Gara Usage Date:** Corrected the claim that Larry Gara dated popular usage of the term to "around 1804" to "during the 1830s and 1840s." The terminology depended on the advent of actual steam railroads in the late 1820s/1830s.
* **William Still Record Counts:** Updated the count of documented individuals in Still's journal from "485" to "649" (the exact count recorded in his Vigilance Committee journal between 1852 and 1857) and noted that his 1872 published book *The Underground Railroad* documented approximately 800 freedom seekers.
* **Robert Purvis Residence Details:** Clarified that while Purvis lived at Ninth and Lombard Streets in the city during the 1830s, his residence featuring the famous secret room/trapdoor in the basement was his country farm in Byberry (Northeast Philadelphia), where he moved in the 1840s.
* **Henry "Box" Brown Box Dimensions and Arrival:** Clarified the dimensions of Henry "Box" Brown's crate (3 feet 1 inch long, 2 feet wide, 2 feet 8 inches deep) and noted his arrival date in Philadelphia on March 24, 1849 following his shipment on March 23.
* **Passmore Williamson Title:** Specified that Williamson served as secretary of the Acting Committee of the Pennsylvania Abolition Society.
