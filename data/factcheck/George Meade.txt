* **Infobox / Civic Roles & Lead/Personal Life sections:** Changed the start date of Meade's appointment as a Fairmount Park commissioner from 1866 to 1868 (and serving as Commission President starting in 1869). The Pennsylvania General Assembly did not establish the Fairmount Park Commission until March 26, 1867; Meade was appointed in 1868.
* **Lead section & Career section:** Corrected the date of Ulysses S. Grant's appointment as Lieutenant General and General-in-Chief from "April 1864" to "March 1864" (Lincoln formally commissioned Grant on March 9, 1864, and assigned him command of all Union forces on March 10–12, 1864).
* **Early life and education section:** Replaced "re-enlisted" with "was re-appointed" regarding Meade's 1842 return to military service. Enlistment applies to enlisted personnel, whereas officers receive a military commission/appointment.
* **Command Hierarchy Diagram (1864–1865):** Changed `(Hancock/Barlow)` under II Corps to `(Hancock/Humphreys)`. Francis C. Barlow was a division commander within the II Corps, whereas Major General Andrew A. Humphreys succeeded Winfield Scott Hancock as commander of the II Corps in November 1864.
