* **Corrected marriage year for Sarah Bass**: Changed the year Richard Allen married Sarah Bass from 1804 to 1802 in both the Infobox and the "Personal life" section. Historical records show Richard Allen and Sarah Bass were married on August 18, 1802.
* **Corrected the date of the St. George's Church Walkout**: Updated the date of the walkout from 1787 to circa 1792 in the Lead section and Career section. Although Allen later recalled the walkout as occurring in 1787 in his autobiography, church records and historical research show that the gallery addition at St. George's was not constructed until 1791–1792, placing the incident in late 1792.
* **Corrected specie conversion for freedom purchase**: Clarified that $2,000 in Continental currency was equal to £60 (sixty Pennsylvania pounds) in gold or silver specie, rather than "$60".
* **Clarified family members sold by Benjamin Chew**: Clarified that Chew sold Allen's mother and her four children total (Richard and three siblings), eliminating ambiguity regarding the number of children involved.
