* Changed "St. Frances Academy" to "House of the Good Shepherd" in the "Early life and education" section: The Catholic reform institution Holiday was sent to in Baltimore for truancy during her youth was the House of the Good Shepherd.
* Changed "Cointreau's" to "Monette's" in the "Career" section: John Hammond famously discovered Billie Holiday performing at Monette's Supper Club in Harlem in 1933.
* Corrected the marriage history in the "Personal life" section: Holiday was married twice (to Jimmy Monroe in 1941 and Louis McKay in 1957). Her relationship with trumpeter Joe Guy occurred in the mid-1940s (not 1951), and they were never legally married.
* Corrected the location of the May 1947 federal narcotics raid/arrest in the Lead and "Personal life" sections from Philadelphia to New York City: The raid and arrest occurred at her New York City residence, though her trial subsequently took place in federal court in Philadelphia.
