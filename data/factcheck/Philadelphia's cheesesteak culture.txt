* **Infobox & Origins (Cheese Origin Date & Key Figures)**: Changed the addition of cheese date from "1950s" to "1940s". Cheese (provolone) was first added to the steak sandwich by Pat's manager/cook Joe "Cocky" Lorenza in the 1940s, whereas Cheez Whiz was introduced later in 1952.
* **Origins (Pat's Establishment Year)**: Corrected the text and ASCII chart to show that Pat & Harry Olivieri operated at the 9th & Passyunk location from 1930 onward, rather than opening a new location in 1940.
* **Origins (Demographic & Kosher Claims)**: Removed the incorrect narrative asserting that cheese was originally omitted due to local Jewish patrons adhering to kosher laws, as well as the apocryphal story about warming cans of Cheez Whiz with paint stirrers on the grill to prevent kosher contamination. South Philadelphia's 9th Street Italian Market neighborhood was overwhelmingly an Italian-American enclave, and non-kosher beef stands were already non-kosher regardless of cheese.
* **Development (2005 Super Bowl Bet)**: Corrected the claim regarding Governor Mitt Romney refusing to eat Rendell's wagered cheesesteak due to high cholesterol. Romney accepted and ate the cheesesteaks delivered by Rendell following the Patriots' victory in Super Bowl XXXIX.
* **Development (ASCII Chart)**: Updated Pat's establishment date from 1940 to 1930 in the comparison diagram.
