* **Corrected claims regarding Lydia H. Sigourney as a European correspondent (Lead and Career sections):** Lydia H. Sigourney was a Connecticut-based American poet and regular contributor to *Godey's Lady's Book*, not a European correspondent sending London dispatches on wedding gowns or Christmas trees. References to her acting as a European correspondent sending London dispatches were removed and replaced with accurate descriptions of how the magazine adapted European prints and commentary.
* **Updated Career diagram:** Changed `• European Reports: Lydia Sigourney` to `• Domestic advice & cultural commentary` to reflect accurate sources of content.
* **Corrected magazine ownership history post-1877 (Career section):** Louis Antoine Godey did not sell the magazine directly to "John Hill Seyes Haulenbeek" in 1877. In 1877, Godey sold the magazine upon his retirement; J. H. (John H.) Haulenbeek acquired *Godey's Lady's Book* years later, in 1892, when the magazine moved its headquarters to New York.
