* **Infobox & Section 5 (Resting place / Death):** Corrected the resting place from "Christ Church Burial Ground" (located at 5th and Arch Streets) to "Christ Churchyard" (located at 2nd and Market Streets), where the Morris family vault is located at Christ Church itself.
* **Section 4 (Financing the Revolutionary War):** Corrected the timeline regarding Congress evacuating Philadelphia. The original text stated Congress retreated to Baltimore when the British occupied Philadelphia in late 1777. In fact, Congress fled to Baltimore in December 1776; during the British occupation of September 1777, Congress fled to Lancaster and then York, Pennsylvania.
* **Section 6 (Memorials and Honors - Geographic Names):** Replaced "Morris County, New Jersey" and "Morrisonville, New York" with places named after Robert Morris ("Morrisville, Pennsylvania" and "Mount Morris, New York"). Morris County, NJ was established in 1738 and named for Royal Governor Lewis Morris (no relation/predating Robert Morris), while Morrisonville, NY was named after Nathaniel Morrison.
