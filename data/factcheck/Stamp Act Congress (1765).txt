* **Section 3 (Background):** Removed Joseph Fox from the list of Pennsylvania delegates. Pennsylvania sent three delegates (John Dickinson, John Morton, and George Bryan). Joseph Fox was the Speaker of the Pennsylvania Assembly who signed the instructions for the delegates, but he was not himself a delegate.
* **Section 4 (Course of events):** Corrected the name "Joseph Ogden" to "Robert Ogden" of New Jersey, who was Speaker of the New Jersey Assembly and famously refused to sign the congress's petitions alongside Timothy Ruggles.
