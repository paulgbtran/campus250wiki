* **Career**: Removed the statement that "Forget Him" earned Rydell a Grammy Award nomination. While written by Tony Hatch and reaching No. 4 on the Billboard chart, Rydell was never nominated for a Grammy Award.
* **Legacy**: Corrected the street renaming date and official street name from "In 2013, the City of Philadelphia officially designated the block of South 11th Street where he grew up as 'Bobby Rydell Boulevard'" to "In September 2022, the City of Philadelphia officially designated the 2400 block of South 11th Street where he grew up as 'Bobby Rydell Way.'" The street dedication occurred posthumously in September 2022.
