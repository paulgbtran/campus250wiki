* In the **Background** section, changed "agreed to syndicate the show nationally" to "agreed to carry the show nationally on the ABC television network." In television broadcasting terminology, "syndication" refers to selling programs directly to individual independent stations outside a major network. *American Bandstand* debuted in 1957 as a network broadcast pick-up on ABC, not a syndicated program (the show did not enter first-run syndication until 1987).
* In the **Aftermath** section, changed "The national syndication of American Bandstand..." to "The national network broadcast of American Bandstand..." to maintain historical accuracy regarding its ABC network distribution.
