* **Mentor Clarification (Bushnell Keeler):** Corrected the reference to Bushnell Keeler. Keeler was an artist and mentor who advised Lynch during his high school years in Alexandria, Virginia, not an instructor at PAFA.
* **Residence Address Corrected:** Updated Lynch's residential address in Philadelphia to the 12-room house he bought at 2416 Poplar Street (Fairmount/Francisville) in 1967, where he lived with Peggy Reavey and where daughter Jennifer Lynch was born in 1968, rather than a townhouse in Callowhill.
* **Award Correction for *Six Men Getting Sick (Six Times)*:** Corrected the award won by *Six Men Getting Sick (Six Times)* in 1967 from the William Emlen Cresson Foreign Traveling Scholarship to PAFA's annual student prize for experimental painting and sculpture (the Dr. William S. Biddle Cadwalader Prize).
* **AFI Grant & Timeline Correction:** Clarified that the American Film Institute (AFI) grant awarded following *The Alphabet* was used to make his short film *The Grandmother* (1970) in Philadelphia, and the success of *The Grandmother* subsequently led to his move to Los Angeles to join the AFI Conservatory in 1971.
