* **Infobox – Presiding Officers**: Removed Henry Middleton, who served as President of the *First* Continental Congress in October 1774, but was not a presiding officer of the Second Continental Congress. Formatted and listed the full names of the five presidents of the Second Continental Congress (Peyton Randolph, John Hancock, Henry Laurens, John Jay, and Samuel Huntington).
* **Infobox – Key Participants**: Removed Joseph Galloway. Galloway was a delegate to the First Continental Congress (1774) but refused to attend the Second Continental Congress in 1775 due to his Loyalist principles.
* **Section 5 (Aftermath) – Wartime Evacuations**: Clarified the timeline of Congress's evacuations. The text previously grouped Baltimore, Princeton, and New York City into the fall 1777 evacuation following the Battle of Brandywine. In fact, Congress fled to Baltimore earlier (December 1776 – March 1777), whereas the fall 1777 evacuation took delegates to Lancaster and York, Pennsylvania (September 1777 – June 1778).
