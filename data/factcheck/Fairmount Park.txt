* **Infobox Area:** Corrected area for East and West Fairmount Park from ~4,500 acres to ~2,050 acres. (~4,500 acres refers to the combined area including Wissahickon Valley Park, while East and West Fairmount Park specifically comprise ~2,050 acres).
* **2010 Merger / Governance Reform:** Corrected the text claiming Wissahickon Valley Park was designated an "independent entity" following the 2010 dissolution of the Fairmount Park Commission. Wissahickon Valley Park remains part of the municipal park system administered by Philadelphia Parks & Recreation (PPR).
* **Fairmount Water Works Engineer:** Removed John Davis as co-engineer for Fairmount Water Works. Frederick Graff was the primary designer and chief engineer of the Fairmount Water Works (John Davis was an engineer on the earlier 1801 Centre Square Water Works).
* **Robert Morris's Estate Name:** Corrected the original name of Robert Morris's estate at Lemon Hill from "Springettsbury" to "The Hills". (Springettsbury was the name of the Penn family's proprietary manor).
* **Woodford History:** Corrected the attribution of the 1771 expansion of Woodford. Original builder William Coleman died in 1769; the second story was added c. 1771 by subsequent owner David Franks.
