* **Founding Dedication**: Corrected "in memory of her parents" to "in honor of her father" (Cyrus H. K. Curtis). Cyrus Curtis was still alive in 1924 (he passed away in 1933).
* **Rosario Scalero's Role**: Corrected description from "vocal teacher" to "composition teacher." Rosario Scalero was a prominent teacher of composition and music theory at Curtis whose students included Samuel Barber and Gian Carlo Menotti.
* **1724 Locust Street Mansion Name**: Corrected "Oliver De Lancey Scull Mansion" to "Alfred C. Harrison Mansion," which is the actual historical name of the property at 1724 Locust Street.
* **Field Concert Hall Pipe Organ Specifications**: Corrected "5-manual, 116-rank" to "3-manual." The 1927 Skinner pipe organ (Opus 649) in Field Concert Hall is a 3-manual organ.
* **Lenfest Hall Address**: Corrected address from "1608 Locust Street" to "1618 Locust Street."
