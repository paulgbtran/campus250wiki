* **Corrected myth regarding window status and security guards in Course of events**: Changed "Sentries were stationed at the doors, and the hall's windows were nailed shut..." to "Doorkeepers were stationed at the doors, and the hall's windows were kept closed..." Historical accounts confirm windows were kept closed to preserve secrecy, but claims that they were "nailed shut" are popular myth.
* **Clarified timeline of the Great Compromise in Course of events**: Changed "proposed the Great Compromise (or Connecticut Compromise) in mid-July" to "proposed the Great Compromise (or Connecticut Compromise) in June, which was adopted in mid-July." Roger Sherman introduced the compromise proposal on June 11, 1787; it was formally adopted on July 16, 1787.
* **Corrected leadership of the Committee of Style and Arrangement in Course of events**: Clarified that the committee was chaired by William Samuel Johnson, while Gouverneur Morris served as the committee's principal drafter and stylist.
