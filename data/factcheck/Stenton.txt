* **Lead Section & Revolutionary War Section**: Corrected the description of Dinah's legal status at the time of the 1777 Revolutionary War incident. Dinah was manumitted (freed) by the Logan family in 1776, prior to the British soldiers' arrival at Stenton in late 1777. The text originally stated she was enslaved during the incident and was granted her freedom after the war as a reward, which is historically inaccurate.
