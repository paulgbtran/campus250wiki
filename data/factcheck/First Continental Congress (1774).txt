* **Infobox - Key Figures**: Removed Benjamin Franklin. Franklin was residing in England as a colonial agent in 1774 and did not attend or serve as a delegate to the First Continental Congress; he returned to America in May 1775 and joined the Second Continental Congress.
* **Infobox, Lead Section, and Course of Events - Delegate Count**: Corrected total delegate count from 56 to 55. Exactly 55 delegates attended the First Continental Congress (the number 56 is often confused with the 56 signers of the Declaration of Independence in 1776).
* **Background Section - Delegate Housing**: Removed the erroneous assertion that institutions like the Walnut Street Prison, Pennsylvania Hospital, and British Barracks provided housing/lodging for attending delegates. Clarified that delegates stayed in local taverns, inns, boarding houses, and private residences across Philadelphia.
