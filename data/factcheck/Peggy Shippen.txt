* **Lead Section**: Removed the unverified assertion that Shippen was the "highest-paid female spy of the American Revolutionary War." While she received a pension from King George III for her services, calling her the "highest-paid female spy" is an inaccurate/historical mischaracterization.
* **Early life and education**: Clarified Major John André's role during the British occupation of Philadelphia (1777–1778). At that time, André served as aide-de-camp to Major General Charles Grey, rather than General Howe's chief of intelligence (André was appointed head of secret intelligence by Sir Henry Clinton later in 1779 in New York).
* **Early life and education**: Corrected the assertion that Peggy Shippen and her sisters attended and participated in the *Mischianza*. While André drew sketches for their costumes as "Ladies of the Blended Rose," Judge Edward Shippen prohibited his daughters from attending the event due to propriety/costume concerns.
