* **National Historic Landmark Designation Date**: Corrected the designation date from October 15, 1966, to April 15, 1970 (October 15, 1966, was the date it was listed on the National Register of Historic Places).
* **Duration as Tallest Structure**: Updated "nearly half a century" to "over half a century" in the Lead section, as the 196-foot spire remained the tallest structure in North America for 56 years (1754–1810).
* **Revolutionary War Bell Evacuation Location**: Corrected the destination where the church bells were hidden in 1777 from Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, to Allentown, Pennsylvania (they passed through Bethlehem en route to being hidden under Zion Reformed Church in Allentown).
* **Burial Location of Robert Morris**: Removed Robert Morris from the bulleted list of individuals buried at the Christ Church Burial Ground (5th and Arch Streets) and noted his correct burial location in the churchyard adjacent to the main church building on 2nd Street (in the Bishop William White family vault).
