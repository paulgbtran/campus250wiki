* **Infobox & Lead Section / Legacy:** Corrected the landmark designation. Grumblethorpe was listed on the National Register of Historic Places (NRHP) on March 16, 1972 (NRIS #72001153) and is a contributing property to the Colonial Germantown Historic District (a National Historic Landmark district). It was not individually designated a National Historic Landmark on November 27, 1967 (that date applies to nearby Cliveden).
