* **Construction Period (Infobox, Lead, Architecture):** Corrected the construction dates from `1818–1824` to `1819–1824`. Although William Strickland won the architectural competition in 1818, physical ground breaking and cornerstone placement occurred in April 1819.
* **Bank War Rally and Petition Numbers (History):** Removed inaccurate petition numbers and the erroneous statement that 60,000 people protested in West Philadelphia (which was rural farmlands in 1832). Corrected the location of the major post-veto protest meeting to the State House Yard (Independence Square) in Center City Philadelphia.
* **Charles Willson Peale's Year of Death (Cultural Significance):** Corrected Peale's year of death from `1828` to `1827` (he died on February 22, 1827).
* **Peale Museum Auction Date (Cultural Significance):** Corrected the date of the public auction of Peale's collection from `1848` to `1854` (the historic auction occurred in October 1854).
* **George Washington Mask Artifact Description (Cultural Significance):** Changed references to "George Washington's authentic death mask" to "George Washington Life Mask." No death mask was ever taken of George Washington; the mask on display in the portrait gallery is a copy of Jean-Antoine Houdon's 1785 life mask made at Mount Vernon.
