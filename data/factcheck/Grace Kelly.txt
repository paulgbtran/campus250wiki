* **Early life and education:** Corrected Margaret Katherine Majer Kelly's role at the University of Pennsylvania. She was an instructor of physical education and the first director of *women's* athletics, rather than a full professor and head of the entire university athletics department.
* **Personal life:** Corrected the statement regarding Grace Kelly's citizenship. Contrary to common belief, she did not relinquish her United States citizenship upon marrying Prince Rainier III; she maintained dual U.S. and Monégasque citizenship.
