* **Corrected inventor attribution for the rotary tablet machine:** Removed the reference stating that chemist Henry Bower was an employee of John Wyeth & Brother who invented the rotary tablet press in 1872. Henry Bower was an independent Philadelphia chemical manufacturer (famous for pioneering purified commercial glycerin production in 1858), whereas the rotary tablet machine was introduced directly by John Wyeth & Brother.
* **Corrected Argyrol launch date:** Updated the launch year of Argyrol from 1899 to 1902, when Albert C. Barnes and Hermann Hille synthesized and introduced the antiseptic compound.
* **Corrected GlaxoSmithKline merger year:** Updated the merger completion year of Glaxo Wellcome and SmithKline Beecham to form GlaxoSmithKline from 2001 to 2000 (December 2000).
