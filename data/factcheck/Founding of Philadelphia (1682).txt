* **Course of events**: Corrected "white and dark quahog shell beads" to "white whelk and dark quahog shell beads". Traditional wampum consists of white beads crafted from whelk shells and dark purple beads crafted from quahog clam shells; quahog shells do not produce the white beads.
* **Aftermath**: Corrected the current preservation location of the Penn Wampum Belt to the Historical Society of Pennsylvania (HSP). The belt was gifted directly to HSP in 1857, which owns the artifact.
