* **Infobox & Origins Section (Founders & Architects)**: Removed William Strickland from the list of founders, and removed Strickland and Thomas Ustick Walter from the list of architects. The four original founders of Laurel Hill Cemetery Company were John Jay Smith, Benjamin W. Richards, Frederick Brown, and Nathan Dunn. While Strickland and Walter submitted competing proposals for the gatehouse entrance design, John Notman won the competition and was the sole architect responsible for the gatehouse and initial chapel.
* **Lead Section & Preservation Section (NHL Claim)**: Corrected the statement that Laurel Hill was "the first cemetery in the United States to be designated a National Historic Landmark." Several cemeteries were designated prior to Laurel Hill (1998), including Spring Grove Cemetery (1993) and Mount Auburn Cemetery (1997).
* **Site Selection & Early Growth / Notable Interments (David Rittenhouse)**: Removed David Rittenhouse from the list of Revolutionary War figures reinterred at Laurel Hill. Rittenhouse is buried at Old Pine Street Presbyterian Church in Philadelphia, not Laurel Hill.
* **Mid-to-Late 19th Century Expansion (Expansion Dates)**: Corrected the acquisition dates for South Laurel Hill (1849, previously listed as 1844) and Central Laurel Hill (1861, previously listed as 1863) in both text and ASCII diagram to reflect the historical expansion record accurately.
