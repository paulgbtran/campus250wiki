* **Religious Background of William Lewis**: Corrected "Lewis was an ardent Quaker" to "Raised in a Quaker family...". While William Lewis was born into a Quaker family, he was disowned by the Society of Friends due to his active involvement in political office and military matters during the Revolutionary War period.
* **Greek Revival Wing Dimensions**: Corrected "single-story wings" to "two-story wings" in both the *Hemphill Expansion* subsection and the *Architecture* section. The wings added by Joseph Hemphill around 1828 are two stories tall.
* **Exterior Material Description**: Updated the description of the Federal core's exterior from exposed "Flemish-bond red brick" to "brick coated in smooth white stucco" to reflect the actual historic finish of the mansion.
