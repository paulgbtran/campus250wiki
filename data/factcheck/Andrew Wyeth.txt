* **Medium Correction (*Christina's World*)**: Changed "His 1948 canvas *Christina's World*" to "His 1948 painting *Christina's World*". *Christina's World* was executed using tempera on a gessoed hardboard/panel, not canvas.
* **Brandywine School Founder Correction**: Clarified that the "Brandywine School" (or tradition) was established by illustrator Howard Pyle (N.C. Wyeth's teacher), rather than N.C. Wyeth himself.
* **Parentage / Biography Correction (Betsy James)**: Corrected Betsy James's father's profession from "local newspaper editor" to "commercial artist" (Merle James was an artist and art director at the Roycroft Press).
* **Presidential Medal of Freedom Presenter Correction**: Corrected the conferring president of the 1963 Presidential Medal of Freedom from John F. Kennedy to Lyndon B. Johnson. Although President Kennedy selected Wyeth as a recipient in 1963, the medal was conferred by President Johnson in December 1963 following Kennedy's assassination.
