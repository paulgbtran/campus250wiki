* **Lead Section**: Changed "silver half dismes (dimes)" to "silver half dismes (half dimes)" to correctly define a half disme as a five-cent piece rather than a ten-cent dime.
* **Course of Events (Expansion to Silver and Gold)**: Corrected the statement regarding Thomas Jefferson's involvement in lowering the officer bond requirements in March 1794. Jefferson retired as Secretary of State at the end of December 1793; Director David Rittenhouse directly petitioned Congress and President Washington to resolve the issue.
* **Course of Events (Expansion to Silver and Gold)**: Removed the false claim that Joseph Hiester was David Rittenhouse's successor as Mint Director and that he died in the yellow fever epidemics. Joseph Hiester was a Pennsylvania military officer and later Governor of Pennsylvania (who died in 1832). Clarified that Engraver Joseph Wright died during the 1793 yellow fever epidemic and Mint Treasurer Dr. Nicholas Way died during the 1797 epidemic. Added accurate context that Rittenhouse resigned due to ill health in June 1795 and was succeeded briefly by Henry William de Saussure, followed by Elias Boudinot.
