* **Corrected Postal History Claim:** Revised claims stating that the Merchants' Exchange post office was the first in the United States to sell official postage stamps. The first official U.S. postage stamps were issued and sold in New York City on July 1, 1847. The post office inside the Merchants' Exchange Building was the first location in *Philadelphia* to sell official U.S. postage stamps.
* **Corrected Public Access and Exhibit Claims:** Removed claims in the Infobox, Lead, and Cultural Significance sections asserting that the building features a public ground-floor museum/exhibit with open operating hours (Monday–Friday 8:30 am–4:30 pm). According to National Park Service documentation, the building functions strictly as administrative offices for Independence National Historical Park and is closed to the general public. Updated the text to reflect exterior viewing availability instead.
