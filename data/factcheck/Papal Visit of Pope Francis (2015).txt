* **Infobox - Key Figures**: Updated "Governor Tom Corbett" to "Governor Tom Wolf." Tom Wolf took office in January 2015 and served as Governor of Pennsylvania during the September 2015 papal visit. (Tom Corbett was part of the earlier 2014 planning delegation to Rome, which remains accurately cited in the Background section).
* **Lead Section & Course of Events - Lincoln Lectern Significance**: Corrected the description of the historic lectern used by Pope Francis at Independence Hall. Abraham Lincoln used that lectern during a speech at Independence Hall on February 22, 1861, not for the 1863 Gettysburg Address (which was delivered in Gettysburg, Pennsylvania).
* **Infobox & Aftermath - Economic Impact**: Corrected statements regarding the economic impact. Pre-event estimates originally projected $478 million in economic activity, but post-visit economic studies (such as those conducted by Econsult Solutions) concluded that the visit generated roughly $225 million, as security restrictions and perimeters depressed general commercial activity in downtown Philadelphia during the event.
