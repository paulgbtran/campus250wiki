* **Hall of Fame Induction:** Changed "National Radio Hall of Fame" to "National Sportscasters and Sportswriters Association Hall of Fame" in the Infobox, Lead section, Legacy section, and References section. Kalas was inducted into the NSSA Hall of Fame in 2008; he was not inducted into the National Radio Hall of Fame.
* **Broadcaster Kalas Replaced:** Changed "Bill Campbell" to "Les Keiter" in the Phillies section. Kalas replaced Les Keiter when he joined the Phillies broadcasting team in 1971 (Bill Campbell left the Phillies booth in 1962).
* **2008 World Series Call Quote:** Corrected the transcription of Kalas's call of the final out of the 2008 World Series from *"Brad Lidge offers... swing and a miss!"* to the verbatim call: *"One strike away. Lidge stretches. 0-2 pitch on the way. Swing and a miss!"*.
* **Statue Sculptor:** Changed the artist of the Harry Kalas bronze statue at Citizens Bank Park from "Marc Mellon" to "Lawrence Nowlan".
