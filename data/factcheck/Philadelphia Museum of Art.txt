* **Charter and Opening Dates**: Corrected the charter date of the Pennsylvania Museum and School of Industrial Art from May 10, 1876, to February 27, 1876. May 10, 1876, was the opening date of the Centennial Exhibition; the museum itself officially opened to the public in Memorial Hall on May 10, 1877.
* **Architectural Style**: Corrected the architectural classification of the main building (completed in 1928) from Greek Revival to Neoclassical.
* **School Site Relocation Timeline**: Clarified that the School of Industrial Art moved to its South Broad Street location (Broad and Pine Streets) in 1893, rather than in 1938. In 1938, the museum officially adopted its current name, the Philadelphia Museum of Art, and the school was renamed the Philadelphia Museum School of Industrial Art.
* **Initial Admission Fee**: Corrected the first general admission fee introduced in 1962 from 35 cents to 50 cents.
