* **Infobox - Key Historical Events:** Updated the entry for the Articles of Confederation to specify "Signing (1778) and Entry into Force (1781)". Delegates began signing the Articles at Independence Hall in July 1778; 1781 was when final ratification occurred and they officially went into effect.
* **History Diagram & Architecture Sections:** Corrected references describing the Assembly Room and Supreme Court Chamber as being in the "East Wing" and "West Wing". They are located in the East Room and West Room on the ground floor of the *main building*, whereas the "wings" refer to the attached exterior side buildings/dependencies.
* **Cultural Significance - Abolitionism:** Removed the specific reference to Frederick Douglass speaking on Independence Square in 1844. Douglass was lecturing in New England at that time; anti-slavery rallies were held on Independence Square by various abolitionist leaders, but Douglass was not a featured speaker there in 1844.
* **Cultural Significance - Women's Suffrage:** Corrected the account of the July 4, 1876 demonstration to remove Elizabeth Cady Stanton from the group that interrupted proceedings on Independence Square. Stanton was refused a stage credential by the Centennial Commission and held a separate meeting at the nearby First Unitarian Church; Susan B. Anthony led a delegation of four other suffragists (Matilda Joslyn Gage, Sara Andrews Spencer, Lillie Devereux Blake, and Phoebe Couzins) onto the platform.
