* **Section 4 (Diagram & Text)**: Corrected the NBA Finals appearance year in the diagram and text from 1981 to 1982 (1981–82 season).
* **Section 4 (The 1982–83 Championship Season)**: Corrected the claim that the 76ers "swept through the Eastern Conference bracket" in 1983. The 76ers swept the Knicks (4–0) in the semifinals but dropped one game to the Milwaukee Bucks in the Eastern Conference Finals (4–1) before sweeping the Lakers (4–0) in the Finals.
