* **Removed erroneous nickname "Eve":** Removed instances of "Eve" and "Eve Price" from the article title, infobox, and lead paragraph. The article subject is Philadelphia civic leader Mrs. Evelyn Taylor Price (wife of Eli Kirk Price II), who was not known by the moniker "Eve" (which belongs to the Philadelphia recording artist/actress Eve Jihan Cooper). Added her historical formal name, "Mrs. Eli Kirk Price II," to the infobox and lead section.
* **Corrected commissioning organization of the memorial sundial:** Changed the commissioning entity of the *Evelyn Taylor Price Memorial Sundial* from the "Rittenhouse Square Improvement Association" to the **Flower Market Association** (or Rittenhouse Square Flower Market Association). Historical records and the Association for Public Art confirm that the Flower Market Association commissioned Beatrice Fenton to create the sundial in 1947 to honor its former president.
* **Corrected granddaughter's name:** Corrected "Evie Taylor Price Scott" to **Evelyn Price Scott** throughout the infobox, lead, personal life, and legacy sections. Evelyn Price Scott is the recorded granddaughter who provided commentary for the Association for Public Art's *Museum Without Walls: AUDIO* program.
