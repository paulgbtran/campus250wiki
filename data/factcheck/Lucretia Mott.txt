* **Early life and education**: Corrected "one of eight children" to "one of six children." Thomas Coffin Jr. and Anna Folger had six children (Sarah, Lucretia, Eliza, Mary, Thomas, and Martha).
* **The 1840 World Anti-Slavery Convention**: Corrected venue location from "Exeter Hall" to "Freemasons' Hall" in London, where the June 1840 convention was held on Great Queen Street.
* **The Seneca Falls Convention of 1848**: Removed Susan B. Anthony from the list of leaders using the Seneca Falls platform in 1848. Susan B. Anthony did not attend the 1848 Seneca Falls Convention (she first met Elizabeth Cady Stanton in 1851).
* **Legacy**: Clarified that Mott is depicted as a life-size bronze figure within Lloyd Lillie's multi-figure installation (*The First Wave*) at the Women's Rights National Historical Park visitor center, rather than being a single standalone monumental statue.
