* **Corrected City Council position (Infobox, Lead, and Electoral Politics sections):** Changed references stating Alexander represented "District 5" to reflect that he was elected and served as a **Councilman-at-Large** (one of seven at-large members elected under Philadelphia's newly adopted 1951 Home Rule Charter).
* **Corrected Berwyn desegregation case citation:** Removed the inline case title `(*Boulding v. Tredyffrin/Easttown School District*)`. The consolidated "Tredyffrin/Easttown School District" did not exist in the early 1930s (it was created in 1954), and the legal fight over the desegregation of Berwyn schools involved local township boards and state-level administrative/mandamus actions rather than a suit by that modern district name.
