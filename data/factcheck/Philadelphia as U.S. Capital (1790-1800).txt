* **Section 2 (Lead Section):** Corrected "Congress ratified the Bill of Rights in 1791" to "The Bill of Rights was ratified by the states in 1791". Constitutional amendments in the United States are ratified by state legislatures, not by Congress (Congress proposed the amendments in 1789, and Virginia became the 11th state to ratify on December 15, 1791).
* **Section 4 (The Executive Branch at the President's House):** Corrected the description of legal slavery laws in Pennsylvania. The text previously stated that a 1788 amendment prohibited rotating enslaved people out of state to reset the six-month clock and that Washington knowingly broke the law. In reality, Pennsylvania's 1780 Gradual Abolition Act permitted non-residents to keep enslaved staff for up to six continuous months. Washington exploited this specific statutory condition by sending his enslaved workers out of state before six continuous months elapsed to reset their residency period, thereby legally circumventing emancipation rather than violating an explicit statutory ban.
