* **John Wilkes Booth Artifact Identification**: Corrected references in the Infobox and lead paragraph from "vertebra of John Wilkes Booth" / "a vertebra removed from John Wilkes Booth" to a "tissue specimen" / "tissue specimen removed from John Wilkes Booth." The cervical vertebrae of John Wilkes Booth are preserved at the National Museum of Health and Medicine (NMHM), whereas the Mütter Museum holds a soft tissue specimen removed during Booth's autopsy.
* **Laura Lindgren Role Clarification**: Clarified that Laura Lindgren acted as the editor and publisher (Blast Books) who conceived and produced the Mütter Museum calendars and books, collaborating with fine-art photographers (such as Rosamond Purcell and Olivia Parker), rather than describing her as a fine-art photographer herself.
