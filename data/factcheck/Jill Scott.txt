* **Poetry Collection Title**: Corrected the title of Jill Scott's 2005 published poetry collection in both the Lead Section and the Acting and Literary Accomplishments section from *The Moment Before the Music Begins* to *The Moments, the Minutes, the Hours*.
* **Album Release Date**: Corrected the release date of *Beautifully Human: Words and Sounds Vol. 2* from March 2004 to September 2004.
* **Song/Album Misattribution**: Clarified that "Daydreamin'" was a featured collaboration on Lupe Fiasco's single/album (from *Lupe Fiasco's Food & Liquor*) rather than a single from Scott's own album *The Real Thing: Words and Sounds Vol. 3*.
* **Film Role Identification**: Corrected Scott's character name in the 2014 James Brown biographical film *Get On Up* from "Honey Washington" to James Brown's second wife, Deidre "Dee-Dee" Jenkins.
* **Personal Life Details**: Corrected the name of her former fiancé and tour drummer (father of her son Jett Hamilton Roberts) from the fictional name "Li'Niez Mercer" to Lil' John Roberts.
* **City Honors**: Removed the inaccurate claim that the City of Philadelphia named a section of North 22nd Street in her honor.
