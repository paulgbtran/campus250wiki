* **Infobox & Architecture Section (Architects)**: Corrected the architects listed for the Pine Building. Samuel Rhoads designed the original East Wing (1755), while builder/architect David Evans Jr. designed and constructed the West Wing (1796) and Center Pavilion (1804). Removed Joseph Fox.
* **Architecture Section (Center Pavilion Skylight)**: Removed the statement claiming that the skylight/rotunda of the Center Pavilion was inspired by Thomas Jefferson's design for Monticello. The central skylight was designed functionally by David Evans Jr. to direct overhead sunlight into the surgical amphitheatre for medical operations.
* **Architecture Section (Museum of Nursing History)**: Removed references to a "Museum of Nursing History" in the West Wing adjacent to the amphitheatre. The top floor of the Center Pavilion houses the Surgical Amphitheatre, and there is no "Museum of Nursing History" located in the West Wing. Updated the ASCII layout diagram to accurately reflect historic ward/clinical space.
* **Architecture & Cultural Significance (Thomas Eakins Portrait)**: Removed claims that Thomas Eakins' portrait of Dr. Jacob Mendez Da Costa is located in the President's Office at Pennsylvania Hospital. That portrait is held in the collection of Thomas Jefferson University.
* **Cultural Significance Section (Benjamin West Painting Location)**: Corrected the location description of Benjamin West's *Christ Healing the Sick in the Temple*. Originally housed in a standalone "Picture House" built in 1817 (demolished in 1893), the painting is now displayed inside the Pine Building (Zubrow Auditorium), not a "Gallery Pavilion".
