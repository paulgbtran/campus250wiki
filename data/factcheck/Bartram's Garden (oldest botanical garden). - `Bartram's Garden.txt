* **Lead section**: Corrected "daughter Ann Bartram Carr" to "granddaughter Ann Bartram Carr". Ann Bartram Carr was the daughter of John Bartram Jr., making her John Bartram's granddaughter.
* **Career - The second generation**: Changed "In the summer of 1784, delegates attending the Continental Congress..." to "In the summer of 1787, delegates attending the Constitutional Convention...". The famous delegation visit occurred in July 1787 during the Constitutional Convention in Philadelphia.
* **Legacy - Historic designation and living collections**: Changed the date for André Michaux sending the Yellowwood tree (*Cladrastis kentukea*) from 1784 to 1796. Michaux did not arrive in North America until late 1785 and encountered the tree in Tennessee in 1796, sending specimens to the Bartrams that same year.
