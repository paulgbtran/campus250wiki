* **Course of events (The 1913 World Series)**: Corrected the claim that Jack Coombs pitched in Games 4 and 5 of the 1913 World Series. Jack Coombs missed nearly the entire 1913 season due to severe typhoid fever/spinal infection and did not pitch in the 1913 postseason. Added mention of 21-year-old rookie Bullet Joe Bush, who pitched the complete game win in Game 3, and clarified that Chief Bender pitched Game 4 (a 6–5 win) while Eddie Plank pitched Game 5.
