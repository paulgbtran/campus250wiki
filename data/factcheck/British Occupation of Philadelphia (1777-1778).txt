* **Timing of Howe's embarkation/landing:** Corrected the text from stating Howe embarked in August to clarifying that the British army embarked and sailed from New York in July 1777, arriving and disembarking at Head of Elk, Maryland, on August 25, 1777.
* **Date of the Battle of Germantown:** Corrected the date of the Battle of Germantown from October 5, 1777, to October 4, 1777.
* **Naming of Fort Billingsport:** Corrected references to "Fort Billings at Billings Point" to its actual historical name, **Fort Billingsport** (located in Billingsport, New Jersey).
* **Timeline of General Howe's resignation:** Corrected the statement that Howe submitted his resignation in April 1778. Howe actually tendered his resignation in October 1777 (following news of Saratoga and frustration with British leadership), and received word that London had accepted it in April 1778.
