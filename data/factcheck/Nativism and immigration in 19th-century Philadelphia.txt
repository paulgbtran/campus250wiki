* **German Society of Pennsylvania and Language Advocacy (Section 4)**: Corrected the claim that the German Society of Pennsylvania adopted English as its official language from 1818 to the 1860s. In reality, the German Society actively worked to preserve German culture and language in Pennsylvania, including petitioning the state legislature to publish state laws in German.
* **City Council Chambers at Independence Hall (Section 5)**: Corrected the statement that Mayor Robert T. Conrad "established" City Council chambers on the second floor of Independence Hall. Philadelphia's City Council had been meeting on the second floor of Independence Hall (the Pennsylvania State House) since 1799. Conrad utilized these existing historic chambers to visually link his administration to Revolutionary memory.
* **Anachronistic Philippine-American War reference (Section 5)**: Removed the reference linking the Page Act of 1875 and the Chinese Exclusion Act of 1882 to the Philippine-American War, which did not occur until 1899–1902. Replaced it with context regarding mid-to-late 19th-century Asian immigration trends.
