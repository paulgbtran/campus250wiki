* **Infobox (Coordinates / Alignment)**: Corrected "Western terminus of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway" to "Eastern terminus of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway." LOVE Park is situated at the southeastern/eastern end of the Parkway at 15th Street and JFK Boulevard; the Philadelphia Museum of Art (Eakins Oval) marks the western terminus.
* **Infobox & Cultural Significance (Key Civic Benefactor)**: Corrected F. Eugene Dixon's title from "Chairman, Philadelphia Art Commission" to "Local businessman and philanthropist" (F. Eugene Dixon Jr. was a prominent Philadelphia philanthropist and sports franchise owner who purchased the sculpture for $35,000 to donate it to the city, but he was not chairman of the Art Commission).
* **Cultural Significance (Sculpture Conservation ASCII Box)**: Corrected the color mapping diagram. The *LOVE* sculpture's original 1976 color scheme features red front faces, deep purple sides/interiors (which had mistakenly been repainted blue in prior decades), and green backing/rear faces. The original box had swapped the purple and green placement between the side edges and backing.
