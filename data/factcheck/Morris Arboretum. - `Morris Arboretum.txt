* **Removed Dawn Redwoods from Founders' Introductions**: Replaced "dawn redwoods" with "katsura trees (*Cercidiphyllum japonicum*)" under the **Career** section. Dawn redwoods (*Metasequoia glyptostroboides*) were believed extinct until living specimens were discovered in China in 1941 and introduced to North America via the Arnold Arboretum expedition in 1947–1948. Because John Morris died in 1915 and Lydia Morris died in 1932, the founders could not have collected or introduced dawn redwoods during their lifetimes.
* **Corrected John Blair's Title/Affiliation**: Removed the erroneous description of landscape architect John Blair as the "former superintendent of Fairmount Park." Blair was a Scottish landscape designer who had previously worked on major park projects in Canada (such as Victoria Park in St. Catharines, Ontario) and Chicago, but was never superintendent of Philadelphia's Fairmount Park.
