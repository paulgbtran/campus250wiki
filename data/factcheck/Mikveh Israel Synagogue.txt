* **Corrected Cemetery Location & Initial Land Grant:** Removed references to an initial plot on Walnut Street and subsequent reinterment to Spruce Street. Nathan Levy petitioned for and was granted land directly on Spruce Street (between 8th and 9th Streets) starting with his 1738 request and formal 1740 grant.
* **Clarified Newspaper Public Notice Author:** Changed "prominent local religious leader" to Nathan Levy himself, who placed a famous notice in Benjamin Franklin's *Pennsylvania Gazette* in 1751 offering a reward to stop vandalism before constructing the protective brick wall.
* **Corrected References to Cemetery Property:** Replaced inaccurate mentions of damage to a "sanctuary structure" at the Spruce Street site. The 8th and Spruce site is strictly a historic cemetery; Mikveh Israel's formal synagogue sanctuary buildings were built at distinct locations elsewhere in Philadelphia (e.g., Cherry Street, 4th Street).
* **Corrected Terminology and Spelling:** Fixed minor spelling variations (e.g., "Mathilda Hoffman" to "Matilda Hoffman", "Hebrew veterans" to "Jewish veterans").
