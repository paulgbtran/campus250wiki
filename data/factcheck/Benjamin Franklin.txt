* **Early Life and Education Section**: Corrected Franklin's age when authoring the *Silence Dogood* essays from "fourteen-year-old" to "sixteen-year-old". Franklin was born in January 1706 and wrote the essays in 1722 (published beginning in April 1722).
* **Legacy Section**: Corrected the naming year of the Benjamin Franklin Parkway from "1737/1937" to "1937". The parkway was designed and constructed in the early 20th century (originally known as the Fairmount Parkway) and was officially named the Benjamin Franklin Parkway in 1937.
