* **Name Correction:** Removed the middle name "Dale". Rob McElhenney's legal birth name is Robert McElhenney III.
* **Education Correction:** Removed Temple University from his education history. McElhenney did not attend Temple University as a student; his father worked there as an HR administrator.
* **Pilot Concept Correction:** Clarified that the original self-shot pilot (*Charlie Has Cancer*) was set in Los Angeles and focused on struggling actors. The Philadelphia bar setting was introduced after FX picked up the show.
* **Record-Breaking Season Correction:** Updated the milestone date for *It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia* becoming the longest-running live-action sitcom. It officially broke the record (surpassing *The Adventures of Ozzie and Harriet*) with its 15th season in 2021, rather than its 16th season in 2023.
* **Mac's Tavern Opening Date:** Updated the opening year of Mac's Tavern from 2009 to May 2010.
* **Docuseries Role Correction:** Clarified McElhenney's role on *Welcome to Wrexham* as executive producer/star rather than co-creator.
