* **Personal Life section:** Corrected the reference to Washington Irving's connection. Rebecca Gratz did not have a brother named Thomas Gratz. Washington Irving was connected to the family as a friend and through his engagement to Rebecca's close friend, Matilda Hoffman.
* **Personal Life section:** Corrected "Rachel Gratz Hoffman" to "Rachel Gratz Moses". Rachel Gratz married Solomon Moses in 1806; when she died in 1823, Rebecca raised her sister's nine children. (Matilda Hoffman was a close friend of Rebecca, not her sister).
