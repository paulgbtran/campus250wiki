* **Lewis and Clark Scientific Training (Infobox, Lead, Development, Diagram):** Corrected references stating both Meriwether Lewis and William Clark were trained in Philadelphia in 1803–1804. Meriwether Lewis alone traveled to Philadelphia in the spring of 1803 to undergo scientific instruction under scholars such as Benjamin Rush, Benjamin Smith Barton, and Caspar Wistar.
* **Context of the APS 1743 Founding (Lead & Origins):** Clarified that the founding of the American Philosophical Society in 1743 was framed under Franklin's proposal ("A Proposal for Promoting Useful Knowledge among the British Plantations in America") as an inter-colonial network within the British Empire, rather than an "explicit declaration of scientific independence from Great Britain," which would be anachronistic for 1743.
* **Bartram's Botanical Garden Date (Origins Diagram):** Corrected the diagram date entry for the founding of Bartram's Garden from "1740s" to "1728" to accurately align with John Bartram's acquisition of the Kingsessing property in September 1728 and the subsequent text in the section.
* **Earth Inductor Compass in Practical Inventions Diagram (Impact):** Replaced "Earth Inductor Compass" in the diagram branch for practical 18th-century inventions with "Surveying & Astronomical Instruments." The Earth Inductor Compass was invented in the 1920s by Paul R. Heyl and Lyman J. Briggs; placing it under 18th-century practical inventions was chronologically inaccurate.
