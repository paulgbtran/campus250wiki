* **Corrected sale date from Stedman to Powel**: Fixed the statement implying Powel purchased the home following Stedman's imprisonment in 1774. Powel bought the house in August 1769 when Stedman first encountered financial trouble; Stedman was committed to debtors' prison later in 1774.
* **Corrected and expanded history of interior architectural sales**: Clarified that interior room paneling was acquired by both the Metropolitan Museum of Art (1918) and the Philadelphia Museum of Art (1925), rather than exclusively by the Met in 1917.
* **Added preservation details (1931 campaign)**: Credited Frances Wister and the founding of the Philadelphia Society for the Preservation of Landmarks in 1931, which organized the movement to rescue the house from being turned into a parking garage.
