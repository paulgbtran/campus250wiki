The 1900 census schedules are arranged by state or territory and thereunder by county. In some instances the names of large cities also appear. The entries may not be in strict alphabetical order. Be sure to review the listings for the entire state or territory before placing your order.
Entries also include enumeration district (ED) numbers. If you wish to order 

  the census schedules for a minor civil division or geographic area within a 

  particular county or a certain ward within a given city, it is necessary for 

  you to know the enumeration district number assigned to that place. Sometimes 

  the schedule for a county or a city has been filmed on more than one roll, and 

  the correct enumeration district number is needed in order to select the correct 

  roll.
