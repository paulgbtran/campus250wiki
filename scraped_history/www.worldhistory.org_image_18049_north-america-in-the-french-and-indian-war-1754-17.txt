This map illustrates the course and key developments of the French and Indian
(1754–1763), the North American theater of the global Seven Years' War between Great
and France. It shows the territorial struggles, shifting alliances, and decisive battles that reshaped colonial power in the Americas.
The conflict began in 1754, with France, its colonists, and Native allies (referred to at the time as “Indians”) opposing British forces, colonial militias, and the Iroquois Confederacy. Some Indigenous groups and frontier settlers shifted allegiances over the course of the war, responding to changing threats and opportunities. The war concluded with the
