Charles VI and Pierre Salmon in discussion, illumination on parchment by Mazarine Master, included in
Dialogues de Pierre Salmon
A contemporary depiction of King
talking with his royal secretary, Pierre Salmon, at his bedside. Salmon was an influential member of Charles' court and served in a diplomatic capacity during Isabelle of Valois' (Charles' daughter) marriage to King
Richard II of England
. He wrote an advice book for the king in the form of dialogues during his tenure as secretary.
