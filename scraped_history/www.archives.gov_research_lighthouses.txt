This web page provides a brief introductory guide to researching lighthouse buildings and property and their operations and personnel. The most frequently used records relating to lighthouses are in Record Group 26, Records of the U.S. Coast Guard. The series numbers cited below are those shown in Forrest R. Holdcamper, Preliminary Inventory (P.I.) NC-31,
Preliminary Inventory of the Records of the United States Coast Guard
(unpublished, National Archives, 1963).
