Osterburken is the location of the best-preserved
Limes fort in the state of Baden-Württemberg in Germany. It is also home to the Römermuseum Osterburken, which presents finds from Osterburken and the region and provides information about the Romans on the limes. The museum was constructed over a
bathhouse which remains are exposed in the rear part of the museum. A reconstructed stone watchtower (Wp 8/32) with a
, rampart and ditch can also be seen about 1 km south of the fort in the "Förstlein" area.
The Upper German-Raetian Limes forms the frontier of the
