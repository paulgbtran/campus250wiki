Saturday, February 22nd, 10:00 am - 12:00 pm ET, in-person in Washington, DC
Are you interested in receiving support on your National History Day project? Join us for an in-person student workshop at the National Archives. During the workshop, National Archives educators will provide students, grades 6-12, with support refining their topic/project and researching their topic. Archivists will also be available to assist students with identifying records at the National Archives that align with their topic. For questions about the student workshop, please email education@nara.gov.
