This is a marble funerary stele. The
inscription reads:
"Here lies the baby,
Andros, who has a spirit.
He left the troubles of wisdom and effort to his mother and father."
From Aleppo, in modern-day
Period, 4th century CE. (Museum of
Osama Shukir Muhammed Amin
