The White House Liaison Division, part of the Office of Presidential Libraries, regularly works with the White House on matters relating to presidential transitions, public releases of presidential records, and records management questions.
The National Archives plays a
key role in the transfer of textual, electronic, audiovisual records, and artifacts
from the White House at the end of a presidential administration.
The National Archives
coordinates notifications
to the former and incumbent Presidents when it proposes to publicly release presidential records, and makes the notification available to the public pursuant to 44 U.S.C. 2208(a)(1)(B).
The National Archives
provides the incumbent administration with records management guidance and assistance
with transferring presidential records based on its institutional knowledge and expertise. The National Archives has no authority to enforce records management within the White House.
