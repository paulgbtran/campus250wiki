Photographic print of Ludwig Kohn, an injured
soldier, by Reed B. Bontecou, 1865.
Reed B. Bontecou (1824-1907) was an American surgeon who recorded the wounds of Civil
soldiers, often including his own illustrations of bullet trajectories. His photographs were used to verify the degree of injury and help determine post-war pension payments.
