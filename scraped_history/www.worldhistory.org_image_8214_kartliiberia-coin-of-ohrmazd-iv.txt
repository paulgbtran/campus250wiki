Obverse: Ohrmazd IV to the right. Legend in Pehlevi: hrm – aphzu (Ohrmazd
), in front of the figure. Cross behind the figure. This is placed within onefold circle of the dots. 

Reverse: Fire on altar protected by two guardians. Pehlevi inscription of date and name of the mint are unreadable.

Mint: Unknown.
Nominal: Drachm. 2,92 gr.-3,83 gr.
Date: 591-593 or 586-587 (?).
