The National Archives in Washington, DC, holds permanent historical records of federal agencies and courts dating back to 1774. Some of our most frequently requested records include Papers of the Continental Congress, court records (DC District Court and U.S. Supreme Court), Civil War service records and pension files, 19th- and early 20th-century U.S. Navy deck logs, U.S. Coast Guard deck logs, census records, immigration records, land records, lighthouse records, and American Indian records.
