Ottoman sword of the Yatagan type used by Janissaries. Made in
or the Balkans. 1822 CE. Steel blade. Hilt and balde decorated with
, coral. 73.7 cm (29 in) x 5.7 cm (2 1/4 in). Weight: 850 g (1 lb. 14 oz). Collection of the Metropolitan Museum of Art.
