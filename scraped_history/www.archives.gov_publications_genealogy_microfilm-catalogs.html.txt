The National Archives has microfilmed more than 3,000 series of Federal records 

  that contain a wealth of valuable information for genealogists and other researchers. 

  In these records lie many of the details necessary to document the everyday 

  lives of individuals in the past and to trace your own family's history.
A comprehensive catalog that lists the microfilm publications by record group 

  is available, as are catalogs for the Federal population censuses from 1790 

  to 1930 and subject catalogs that list publications by topic and provide roll-by-roll 

  lists of contents.
