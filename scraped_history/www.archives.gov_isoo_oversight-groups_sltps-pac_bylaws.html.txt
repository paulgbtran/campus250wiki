Information Security Oversight Office (ISOO)
The ByLaws issued July 20, 2012 are in accordance with Executive Order 13549, "Classified National Security Information Program for State, Local, Tribal, and Private Sector Entities (SLTPS)." They provide the guidelines established under the SLTPS, its committees, and its entities.
