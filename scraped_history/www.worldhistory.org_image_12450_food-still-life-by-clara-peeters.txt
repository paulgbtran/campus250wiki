A still-life painting by Clara Peeters showing typical dishes of 16-17th century CE northern
. In the centre is a pie with a container behind it for salt and a typical low, round bread loaf in the foreground. The olives and orange would have been luxury items in northern Europe at that time.  Painted c. 1611 CE. (Museo del Prado, Madrid)
