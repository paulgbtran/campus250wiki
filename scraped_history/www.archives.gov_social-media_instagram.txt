Social Media Accounts at the National Archives
Since 1934, The National Archives has documented American moments from wars to celebrations, Presidential dinners to first ladies, and everything in between. Connect with us on Instagram and take a glimpse at the captured moments of today!
Here, we’ll post pictures and videos of behind-the-scenes looks, shots from our exhibits, special events, and more.
