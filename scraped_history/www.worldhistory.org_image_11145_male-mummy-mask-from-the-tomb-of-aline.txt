This is a mummy mask of Aline's husband. His right hand grasps what appears to be a wreath or garland of rose petals. His arms are wrapped in a toga. The eyebrows are painted (not made of glass) while the eyeballs are made of white and black stones. The eyelashes were made of carefully cut
. He is bearded, and the facial expression and hand gestures reflect a humble appearance. 

Stucco on canvas, gilded. From the
period of Egypt, c. 24 CE. It is on display at the Neues Museum, Berlin, Germany. The tomb of Aline was unearthed in 1892 CE by the German archaeologist Richard von Kaufmann (1850 - 1908 CE). A hewn stele carved with a
inscription mentions the name Aline as the owner of the tomb;  the
was named after this woman's name.
