The May book club discussion will focus on A Remarkable Mother by Jimmy Carter. As we approach Mother's Day, let's take some time to reflect on the inspiration and influence Miss Lillian had on her son before he became President of the United States and the role she played during and after his presidency. Copies are available for sale in our museum store or on our website. This virtual event is free and open to the public. Register now on our website to participate in the discussion on May 6 at 7 p.m. ET. Live attendance is encouraged, as these meetings are not recorded.
All events listed in the calendar are free unless noted.
