A short story. When I was a kid, my parents compelled me to read for at least one hour a day, and it turned out to be a huge mistake for them, because I never wanted to stop reading to do my homework. Since then, the activity I have engaged in most frequently has been reading; therefore, when I meet someone new, I introduce myself as a reader.
Through my reading habit, I became a history enthusiast, although my professional career followed a different path. However, in 2020, during the pandemic, I felt the need to reconnect with my passion, so I began my second degree in history (which I will complete in March 2024).
I chose the history of the Portuguese language as the topic of my final project because I enjoy learning new languages and understanding how languages are formed and evolve. In June 2023, I started a specialization in translation in order to connect all my passions in the future.
