An Ottoman sword of state, used during inauguration ceremonies for sultans. The sword was refitted in 1876 CE for the planned inauguration of the Ottoman sultan Murad V (r. 1876 CE). The sword is comprised of a 17th Century CE Iranian steel blade with an 18th Century CE Indian jade grip. Decorated with
and gilded brass mounts of 19th Century CE workmanship, studded with
, jade, diamonds, emeralds, and pearls. The emerald near the top of the scabbard hides a secret compartment containing a gold
marked with the name of Sultan Suleiman the Magnificient (r. 1520–1566 CE). Length: 99.8 cm (39 1/4 in). Weight: 1023 g (2 lb 4 oz). Collection of the Metropolitan Museum of Art.
