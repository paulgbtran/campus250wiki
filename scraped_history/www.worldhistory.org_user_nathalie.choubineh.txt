Nathalie is a translator, researcher, and writer with publications on ancient history, dance in ancient art and history, ethnomusicology, literature, and drama. She holds two BA degrees, both from the University of Tehran, in English Language and Literature and Music, as well as a master's degree in Ancient Mediterranean Civilizations from the University of Leicester, and a PhD in Classics from the University of Reading.
Nathalie's publications include chapters in two books exploring the history, ethnology, and iconography of Persian dance. In collaboration with the Iranian Institute of Anthropology and Culture, she has also written essays on various topics in archaeology and ancient history. She began her voluntary work for the World History Encyclopedia in 2020 as a translator and content writer and has continued her collaboration since then alongside her professional activities in research and teaching.
