This woman's headdress is composed of a double row of carnelian and lapis lazuli beads. Thirteen
leaf-shaped pendants are attached to the beads. The leaves appear to be polar leaves or leaves of the sissoo, or Indian rosewood tree. This jewellery was part of a headdress worn by one of Queen Puabi's attendants and was found inside Puabi's
Period, circa 2600 BCE. From the Royal Cemetery at
, modern-day Iraq. Part of objects allotted to the British Museum from Ur excavation season 1927-1928 CE. (The British Museum, London).
