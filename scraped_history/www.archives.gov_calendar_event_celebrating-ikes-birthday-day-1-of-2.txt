A full weekend is planned for President Eisenhower’s birthday at the Eisenhower Presidential Library and Museum, 200 SE 4th Street, Abilene. The public is invited to participate in an array of activities Friday, October 11 and Saturday, October 12. All events are free and open to the public with the exception of the Legacy Gala set for Saturday evening.
Dwight Eisenhower was born October 14, 1890, in Denison, Texas. He is the only one of the Eisenhower brothers born outside of Dickinson County, Kansas. The family moved back to Abilene, Kansas, when Ike was about two years old. General Eisenhower stated during his homecoming speech following World War II, “the proudest thing I can claim is that I am from Abilene!” Each year the celebratory events are held the weekend closest to his birthday.
