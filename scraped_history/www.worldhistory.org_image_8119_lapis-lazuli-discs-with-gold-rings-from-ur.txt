The lapis lazuli discs were carved and polished. They have two perforations through their diameter. They display a flat surface and a grooved circumflex. Each disc is surrounded by a single hammered
ring with eight perforations. These might well have been attached to material, clothing, or perhaps the fringe of a canopy.
Period, circa 2600 BCE. From the Royal Cemetery at
, modern-day Iraq. Part of objects allotted to the British Museum from Ur excavation season 1927-1928 CE. (The British Museum, London).
