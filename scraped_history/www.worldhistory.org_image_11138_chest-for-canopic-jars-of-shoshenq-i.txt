This calcite-alabaster chest and its lid were made for storing the canopic jars of Shoshenq I (reigned 943-922 BCE), the founder of the 22nd (Bubastite or Libyan) Dynasty of
. The nomen and prenomen cartouches of Shoshenq I are carved on the surface. The precise provenance of Shoshenq I's
is unknown. This relic is the only funerary object linked to Shoshenq I; in 1891 CE,
Isaac donated this canopic chest to the
Museum of Berlin (now part of the Neues Museum). From Egypt.
Third Intermediate Period
. It is on display at the Neues Museum, Berlin, Germany.
