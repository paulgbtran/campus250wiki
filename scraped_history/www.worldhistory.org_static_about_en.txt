World History Encyclopedia is a non-profit organisation that publishes free, high-quality historical content to advance historical literacy and improve history education worldwide. We believe that deeper knowledge of our interconnected human past creates curious, open-minded, and democratic societies.
Our mission is to engage people with cultural heritage and to improve history education worldwide.
History education shapes how we understand ourselves, our societies, and our view of the future. In today’s polarized world,
understanding history isn’t a luxury – it’s a necessity
. Quality history education gives people the tools to recognize bias, resist manipulation, and engage thoughtfully in civic life. It teaches that the past is complex, that narratives can conflict, and that truth must be grounded in evidence.
Yet easy to understand historical resources of academic quality remain out of reach for millions of learners worldwide.
We exist to change that by making comprehensive historical knowledge freely accessible to everyone, helping people develop the critical thinking skills needed to navigate our complex, interconnected world. We believe that access to historical knowledge is a human right that should be extended to all.
World History Encyclopedia (WHE) is a leading non-profit organisation with a global mission. Since our inception in 2009, we have made history education freely accessible to everyone, everywhere. Today, we are proud to be the most-read history publication in the world, with millions of visitors each month from 150+ countries, forming a diverse and interconnected community of history enthusiasts.
Our editorial content is rigorously researched, peer-reviewed, and authored by historians, archaeologists, educators, and scholars from around the globe. This expert authorship contributes to our status as a trusted resource in classrooms and universities, recommended by esteemed institutions such as Oxford University, California State University, and Common Sense Education.
World History Encyclopedia maintains complete editorial independence
, providing objective historical information based on current scholarly consensus, free from political bias or ideological influence.

As a registered non-profit organisation in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Canada, WHE operates through a sustainable funding model that combines advertising revenue, member subscriptions, and donations, allowing us to keep all educational content completely free whilst maintaining our independence and editorial integrity.

Since our founding in 2009, we have grown from a small history website to become
the world's largest and most popular history encyclopedia
. Our reach demonstrates the global need for quality historical education.
Today, we continue expanding our impact through innovative approaches to historical education. We are developing new resources for educators, exploring partnerships with schools and universities, and using technology to make historical knowledge more accessible than ever before. We are expanding our reach by developing a mobile app, ensuring that quality historical content is available whenever and wherever our readers want to learn, including offline reading capabilities for those without consistent internet access.
Our commitment remains unchanged: to provide free, high-quality historical education that builds historical literacy and helps create a more informed, understanding world.
World History Encyclopedia was founded in 2009 by Jan van der Crabben, who recognised that the internet lacked a comprehensive, reliable resource for historical education. Originally launched as Ancient History Encyclopedia, we rebranded in 2021 to reflect our expanded coverage of world history.
From the beginning, our approach has been rooted in the understanding that history is interconnected rather than linear. This philosophy shapes how we present historical knowledge, helping readers understand the complex relationships between different cultures, periods, and events.
As a registered non-profit organisation, we remain committed to transparency and accountability. We publish
detailing how we invest donations and membership revenue in our mission to advance historical literacy worldwide.
