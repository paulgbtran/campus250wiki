Radegund, niece of the King of Thuringia, is brought before King
(r. 511-561), ruler of the Frankish kingdom of Soissons. Chlothar would take Radegund back to Soissons as his spoil of
, and would later make her one of his wives. Illustration by an unknown artist from an 11th-century manuscript.
