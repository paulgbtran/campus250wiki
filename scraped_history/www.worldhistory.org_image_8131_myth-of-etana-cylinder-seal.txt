The six discs above them may represent cheese. Above, a crouching bearded man is working a churn. The right hand of the shepherd carries a whip and there is a staff on his shoulder. He wears a long robe and has a long beard.
Period, circa 2250 BCE. Precise provenance of excavation is unknown. (The British Museum, London).
